What is R2?
Welcome to R2; a biologist friendly open online genomics analysis and visualization application developed by the Koster group at Amsterdam UMC, the Netherlands. You can start exploring the omics data by following the numbered options in the center and menu items at the left side.
For citations, please include the following webcite: 'R2: Genomics Analysis and Visualization Platform (https://r2platform.com)'.

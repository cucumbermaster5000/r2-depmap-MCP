Predefined unpaired Kruskal-Wallis comparison of distributions. Samples are treated as independent primary tumours as described by the source. Batch, age and other confounding are not adjusted in this Version 1 smoke test. No pairwise or subtype/clinical analyses were performed. Missing annotations/expression are reported in the exclusions file. Epsilon-squared is the non-negative rank-based omnibus effect estimate (H-k+1)/(n-k).
